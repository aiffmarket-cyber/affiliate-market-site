Privacy Policy

Affiliate Market — last updated 1 January 2026

Affiliate Market ("the tool") is a private application operated by a single individual ("the operator") to publish posts to social media accounts that the operator owns. It is not offered to the public, has no user registration, and is not used to process anyone else's personal data.

1. Who this covers

The only person whose data the tool handles is the operator. There is no mechanism for any other individual to create an account, sign in, or submit information.

2. What is accessed

When the operator connects an account, the tool accesses only what is needed to publish and measure posts:

  • The operator's own profile identifiers, such as account ID, username, and board or page names
  • Authorisation tokens issued by the platform to the operator
  • Posts created by the tool, and the engagement statistics the platform reports for those posts
  • Product information supplied by affiliate programmes the operator has joined

The tool does not access followers, direct messages, contact lists, or content belonging to other users of any platform.

3. Where data is stored

All data is stored locally, on hardware controlled by the operator, in a local database file. Authorisation tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. There is no hosted backend, no analytics service, and no third-party data processor.

4. What is shared

Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for advertising. Data leaves the operator's machine only when it is sent to a platform's own API in order to carry out an action the operator requested — for example, publishing a post to that platform, or requesting statistics for a post already published there.

The tool may send product descriptions to an AI provider chosen by the operator in order to draft post text. Those descriptions are supplied by merchants and contain no personal data.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is retained for as long as the operator keeps it. Because everything is stored locally, the operator can delete any or all of it at any time by removing the local data files, and can revoke this tool's access from each platform's own settings at any time.

6. Cookies

The tool's interface runs locally and uses a single session cookie to keep the operator signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used. This website itself sets no cookies.

7. Affiliate disclosure

Posts published by the tool contain affiliate links. The operator may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no additional cost to the buyer. Each post includes a disclosure to that effect, as required by the FTC and by the platforms' own policies.

8. Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle data relating to anyone under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
