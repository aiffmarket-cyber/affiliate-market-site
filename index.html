Affiliate Market

A private publishing tool for a single operator's own social accounts.

This is an internal tool. It is not a product, not a service, and has no users other than the person who runs it. It exists to schedule and publish product recommendation posts to social accounts owned by that same person.

What it does

  • Keeps a catalogue of products from affiliate programmes the operator has joined
  • Drafts post copy and composes graphics from merchant-supplied product images
  • Schedules and publishes those posts to the operator's own connected accounts
  • Reads back engagement figures for those same posts to inform what gets posted next

What it does not do

  • It has no sign-up, no accounts for other people, and no way for anyone else to use it
  • It does not read, collect, or store data belonging to any other person
  • It does not sell, share, or transmit data to third parties beyond the platform APIs it publishes to
Affiliate disclosure. Posts published by this tool contain affiliate links. The operator may earn a commission when someone buys through them, at no additional cost to the buyer. Every post carries a disclosure to that effect.
